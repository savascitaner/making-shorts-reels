# 02 - Google Flow Sahne Promptları (9:16 Dikey Format)

**Platform:** Google Flow (Veo Video Generation)  
**En-Boy Oranı (Aspect Ratio):** `9:16` (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)  
**Görsel Stil:** Sinematik belgesel, 1920'ler dönem atmosferi ile modern kozmik kuantum görselleştirmelerinin birleşimi.  

> **Kullanım İpucu:** Google Flow'da her sahneyi üretirken en-boy oranını `9:16` olarak seçin. Karakter tutarlılığı için Einstein ve Bohr görsellerini Flow'un **Characters / Ingredients** sekmesine referans olarak ekleyebilirsiniz.

---

## Sahne 1: Tarihi Yüzleşme (0:00 - 0:20)
* **Sahne Amacı:** 1927 Solvay Konferansı atmosferinde iki dahinin gerilimli bakışması.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Slow dolly-in camera movement toward Albert Einstein and Niels Bohr sitting across from each other at a grand wooden conference table in 1927 Brussels, vintage black-and-white photography coming to life with subtle color grading. Dust motes floating in dramatic shaft of sunlight from a tall window, intense intellectual tension, shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain, cinematic documentary style, 9:16 vertical framing.

## Sahne 2: Kuantum İhtimaller Bulutu (0:20 - 0:35)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Gözlemlenmeden önce belirsiz olan elektron ve yazı-tura metaforu.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Extreme close-up macro shot of a vintage silver coin spinning rapidly in mid-air inside a dark minimalist laboratory, transforming into a glowing translucent quantum probability cloud of blue and gold light particles. Smooth slow-motion camera orbit, volumetric lighting, ethereal sci-fi realism, crisp focus, high contrast, 9:16 vertical composition.

## Sahne 3: Zar Atan Evren (0:35 - 0:50)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Einstein'ın "Tanrı evrenle zar atmaz" sözünün görsel metaforu.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Cinematic low-angle tracking shot of glowing translucent glass dice tumbling in zero gravity through deep space surrounded by swirling nebulae and golden mathematical equations floating in the air. Dramatic cosmic rim lighting, mystical yet scientific atmosphere, photorealistic CGI, 9:16 vertical aspect ratio.

## Sahne 4: Işık Kutusu Düşünce Deneyi (0:50 - 1:15)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Einstein'ın yaylı tartıya asılı ışık kutusu ve tek bir fotonun çıkışı.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Studio macro shot of an intricate brass and glass mechanical box suspended from a delicate spring scale in a dimly lit 1930s study. A tiny mechanical shutter opens and a single brilliant beam of golden photon light shoots out, causing the box to recoil slightly upward. Warm tungsten desk lamp lighting, chalkboards with physics formulas in the soft-focus background, cinematic realism, 9:16 vertical framing.

## Sahne 5: Bohr'un Genel Görelilik Hamlesi (1:15 - 1:35)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Yerçekimi alanında zamanın bükülmesi ve Bohr'un zafer gülümsemesi.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Medium close-up shot of Niels Bohr standing before a large chalkboard covered in chalk equations, a subtle triumphant smile on his face as glowing golden spacetime grid lines warp around a rising brass clock beside him. Warm moody chiaroscuro lighting, vintage academic atmosphere, slow push-in camera, 9:16 vertical format.

## Sahne 6: Kuantum Dolanıklık - Uzaktaki Hayalet Etki (1:35 - 2:20)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Evrenin iki ucunda anında haberleşen dolanık parçacıklar.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Fast-paced cosmic zoom-out from a glowing cyan quantum particle hovering above Earth's horizon, connected by an instantaneous shimmering thread of light stretching across galaxies to an identical magenta particle on the edge of the universe. Both particles pulse simultaneously in vibrant synchronization, deep space lighting, awe-inspiring photorealistic VFX, 9:16 vertical framing.

## Sahne 7: 2022 Nobel ve Modern Kuantum Laboratuvarı (2:20 - 3:00)
* **Sahne Amacı:** Teoriden gerçeğe dönüşen modern kuantum optik deneyleri.
* **Google Flow Prompt:**
  > Sleek cinematic tracking shot gliding across a modern quantum physics optical table filled with precision mirrors, lenses, and glowing violet and blue laser beams splitting photons in a dark high-tech laboratory. Futuristic scientific aesthetic, sharp reflections, shallow depth of field, inspiring finale mood, 9:16 vertical composition.
