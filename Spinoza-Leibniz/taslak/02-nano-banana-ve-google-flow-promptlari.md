# 02 - Nano Banana Pro Görsel Üretim ve Google Flow Video / Dudak Senkronizasyonu Promptları

Bu dosya, 3 dakikalık sinematik Reels videosunu ultra yüksek kalitede üretmek için **Nano Banana Pro (Görsel Üretim)** ve **Google Flow (Sinematik Hareket, Kamera ve Dudak Senkronizasyonu)** araçlarına özel hazırlanmış teknik komutları içerir.

---

## ADIM 1: NANO BANANA PRO - KARAKTER VE SAHNE GÖRSEL PROMPTLARI

*Not: Tutarlılık için tüm görsellerde **9:16 dikey format**, **17. yüzyıl Hollanda Altın Çağı (Rembrandt / Vermeer) ışıklandırması** ve **85mm sinematik lens** estetiği korunmuştur.*

### 1. Sahne: Spinoza Portresi (Yakın Plan - Konuşma Tabanı)
```text
Ultra-realistic cinematic portrait of Baruch Spinoza in 1676, 44 years old, Sephardic Jewish philosopher, pale olive skin, deep dark soulful eyes, shoulder-length wavy dark hair, wearing a simple dark 17th-century Dutch wool coat with a plain white linen collar. Sitting in a dimly lit room in The Hague. Warm candlelight from the left illuminating half of his face, deep Rembrandt chiaroscuro shadows on the right. Subtle optical glass lenses and wooden grinding tools softly blurred on a wooden table in front of him. Shot on 85mm lens, f/1.4, photorealistic skin texture, pores, natural film grain, 8k resolution, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

### 2. Sahne: Leibniz Portresi (Yakın Plan - Konuşma Tabanı)
```text
Ultra-realistic cinematic portrait of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz in 1676, 30 years old, German polymath and diplomat, sharp intelligent eyes, prominent nose, wearing an elaborate dark curly 17th-century Baroque wig, dark velvet doublet with fine lace cravat. Sitting across a wooden table in a candlelit study room. Warm golden candlelight reflecting in his eyes, dramatic shadows, parchment manuscripts and a brass compass softly out of focus in the foreground. Rembrandt lighting style, 85mm cinema lens, f/1.4, hyper-detailed skin texture, authentic historical atmosphere, 8k, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

### 3. Sahne: Mercekten Kırılan Işık (B-Roll / Geçiş Görseli)
```text
Extreme macro cinematic shot of a hand-polished 17th-century glass optical lens held by a philosopher's fingers, warm candlelight passing through the crystal glass and refracting into a subtle golden beam across a dark shadowy room. Dust particles floating in the light beam, shallow depth of field, moody chiaroscuro lighting, photorealistic, 8k, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

### 4. Sahne: İkili Geniş / Omuz Üstü Çekim (Atmosfer)
```text
Over-the-shoulder cinematic shot in a 17th-century Dutch study room at night. Baruch Spinoza and Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz sitting opposite each other at an oak table illuminated by a single flickering tallow candle. Rain streaks visible on a leaded glass window in the dark background. Moody, philosophical, high-contrast Baroque painting aesthetic, 35mm cinema lens, 8k, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

## ADIM 2: GOOGLE FLOW - SİNEMATİK HAREKET, MİMİK VE DUDAK SENKRONİZASYONU PROMPTLARI

*Kullanım Talimatı: Nano Banana Pro'dan aldığınız karakter görsellerini **Google Flow**'a referans kare (Image-to-Video / Lip-Sync) olarak yükleyin ve ilgili ses dosyasını (ElevenLabs v4 çıktısı) bağlayarak aşağıdaki hareket direktiflerini girin.*

### Klip 1: Spinoza - Taş Metaforu (00:00 - 00:18)
```text
[Motion & Lip-Sync Prompt]
Cinematic close-up of Baruch Spinoza speaking calmly and intensely. Natural, subtle lip-sync matching the voiceover audio. Slight forward head tilt as he asks the question, micro-expressions around his dark eyes, slow deliberate blinking. Warm candlelight gently flickers across his cheekbones. Ultra-slow, imperceptible camera push-in (dolly in) towards his eyes. Natural breathing motion in his shoulders. Photorealistic, zero distortion, 24fps cinema motion.
```

### Klip 2: Leibniz - Tutkulu İtiraz (00:18 - 00:55)
```text
[Motion & Lip-Sync Prompt]
Dynamic cinematic close-up of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz reacting with passionate intellectual urgency. Natural, expressive lip-sync, furrowed brow, sharp eye contact, slight firm nod to emphasize his words. His dark Baroque curls move subtly with his head motion. Flickering candle reflection dancing in his pupils. Subtle handheld cinematic camera tension, shallow depth of field, 24fps film cadence.
```

### Klip 3: Spinoza - Doğa ve Zorunluluk Yanıtı (00:55 - 01:40)
```text
[Motion & Lip-Sync Prompt]
Intimate close-up of Baruch Spinoza speaking with serene, unshakeable clarity. Precise, natural mouth movements and micro-expressions on his lips and brow. A soft golden light refraction passes gently across his face as if from a glass lens. Steady, locked-off cinema camera aesthetic with organic breathing movement, 24fps, hyper-realistic skin movement.
```

### Klip 4: Leibniz - Senfoni ve Anlam Kapanışı (01:40 - 02:20)
```text
[Motion & Lip-Sync Prompt]
Emotional cinematic portrait of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz speaking with deep conviction and softened gaze. Gentle, persuasive facial expressions, subtle raised eyebrows on emotional peaks, natural lip synchronization. Warm amber glow from the candle off-screen, slow gentle camera pull-back (dolly out) creating a sense of grandeur and hope. 24fps cinematic motion.
```
